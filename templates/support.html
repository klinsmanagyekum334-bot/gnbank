Our Services & Requirements

💰

Personal & Salary Loans

Process

Submit a completed loan application form along with all supporting documents. Your application will be assessed based on your income, credit history, and debt burden ratio (maximum 50% of net monthly income). Upon approval, a facility agreement is executed, and funds are disbursed into your account.

Requirements

  • Completed loan application form
  • One recent passport-sized photograph
  • Valid Ghana Card (mandatory)
  • Introductory letter from your employer
  • Payslips for the previous three (3) months
  • Employer's undertaking to pay salaries into your account
  • Two (2) third-party guarantors with valid IDs
  • Bank statements (last 3–6 months, if applicable)

Eligibility: Applicant must be actively employed, between 18–57 years of age, and have held an account for at least one month.

🏦

Savings & Current Accounts

Process

Visit any GN Bank branch to complete an account opening form. Once your documents are verified, you can make your initial deposit and activate your account. Savings accounts earn interest, while current accounts are ideal for everyday transactions.

Requirements

  • Fully completed account opening form
  • Valid national ID — Ghana Card only
  • One (1) recent passport-sized photograph
  • Proof of residential or digital address (utility bill, tenancy agreement)
  • Initial deposit (minimum GHS 20 for savings accounts)
  • For joint accounts: IDs of all signatories

Note: Business accounts require additional documents such as business registration certificates and IDs of directors.

🏛️

Government Workers Loan

Process

This loan is designed for public sector employees on the Controller and Accountant General's Department (CAGD) payroll. Submit your application along with the required documents. Your loan amount is determined by your affordability as per the CAGD portal. Repayment is made via salary deduction.

Requirements

  • Completed loan application form
  • Valid Ghana Card
  • One passport-sized photograph
  • Proof of address
  • Introductory letter from employer
  • Payslips for the previous three (3) months
  • Employee number and CAGD mandate number/PIN
  • Must be on the Government of Ghana payroll
  • Age: 18–53 years
  • Minimum net income: GHS 3,000

Benefits: Flexible repayment (6–60 months), no penalty for early repayment, and no monthly account keeping charges.

📜

Collateral Requirements

Accepted Collateral Types

For secured lending, GN Bank accepts the following forms of collateral. The market value of the collateral must be at least 120% of the outstanding loan amount.

  • Landed property (with clear and undisputed title)
  • Motor vehicles (with comprehensive insurance)
  • Cash collateral or fixed deposits
  • Shares and other financial securities
  • Corporate or personal guarantees
  • Life insurance policies

Documentation for Landed Property Collateral

  • Original title deeds / land certificate
  • Valuation report from a bank-approved valuer
  • Official search report from the Lands Commission
  • Mortgage agreement (duly stamped and registered)
  • Consent to mortgage (where applicable)
🏠

Mortgage Loans

Process

Submit your mortgage application along with the required documents. The property will be valued by an approved valuer, and a legal search will be conducted. Upon approval, the mortgage agreement is executed, and the loan is disbursed. Repayment terms range from 5 to 10 years, and the loan must be fully repaid before retirement age.

Requirements

  • Completed mortgage application form
  • Valid Ghana Card (or passport for non-residents)
  • Two (2) passport-sized photographs
  • Payslips for the past three (3) months
  • Proof of continuous employment for two (2) years
  • Offer letter or Sale & Purchase Agreement
  • Valuation report on the property
  • Official search report on the property
  • Vendor's title deeds
  • Consent to Assign/Mortgage
  • Deeds of Assignment from the vendor
  • Ten (10) sun-printed copies of the site plan
  • Spousal consent in writing (if married)
  • Minimum equity contribution: 20%

Eligibility: Minimum age of 21, with loan repayment completed by age 60. A satisfactory credit score is required.
