About GN Bank

Your trusted partner in financial success. Serving our community for over 90 years.

Our Story

Built on a foundation of trust, integrity, and a commitment to community.

Founded in 1997 initially as Group Loan and later changed to GN Bank, GN Bank began as a small community savings institution with a simple mission: to help everyday people achieve their financial dreams. Over the decades, we have grown into a full-service financial institution, yet our core values remain unchanged.

Today, we combine the personalized service of a local bank with the innovative technology of a modern digital bank. Whether you are opening your first savings account, buying a home, or growing a business, we are here to guide you every step of the way.

Empowering Our Community

We believe that when our community thrives, we all thrive. That is why we are dedicated to providing accessible lending solutions for local families and small businesses.

From flexible auto loans to comprehensive home mortgages, our lending products are designed to help you build a better future. We take the time to understand your unique financial situation and work with you to find the right solution.
